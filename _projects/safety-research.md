A key research field is the quantification of road users interactions at complex intersections. The work is based on the following dissertation Yiru Jiao (2025) [doi](https://doi.org/10.4233/uuid:ba7621cb-babf-42d0-85d6-a3ef97d3e0af). The key difference however, is the generalization from car-to-car interaction to interaction of all road users groups and considering different road infrastructure types. 

The following image illustrates the interaction area and proximity resistance field of road users to another. Depending on model parameters and calibration the metric can be used for relative safety evaluations. It can however also be inferred as paramter of road user behavior models.
