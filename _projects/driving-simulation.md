# ISO Standartization
Different driving simulators are suited for different use-cases, as cross-simulator studies in the past
confirmed. The question arises as to how to appropriately describe what driving simulator was used in a simula-
tor study. A working initiative within the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) currently addresses
this issue and is developing a standard to systematically describe driving simulators, or use-case requirements
(planned as ISO 26014). Researchers and practitioners will benefit from a suchlike standard, which will introduce
a better comparability across scientific studies and will allow a more precise communication between requesters
and suppliers.
