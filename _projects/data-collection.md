For road user modelling we require real-world traffic data for model calibration and validation. A current work item is drone and lidar data collection. Because many dection algorithms reach their limits with pedestrian and cyclist detection, we refine state-or-the-art algorithms. While algorithms for drone detecions overall produce high-quality data already, we work on a pseudo-labelling algorithm that uses drone detections as training labels for lidar pointclouds.
