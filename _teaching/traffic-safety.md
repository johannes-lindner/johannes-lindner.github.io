This lecture covers fundamental concepts, data analysis methods, and engineering interventions for improving road traffic safety.


## 1. Fundamentals and Key Indicators
* **Legal Framework:** Safety regulations are anchored in the Road Traffic Act (StVG), Road Traffic Regulations (StVO), Road Traffic Licensing Regulations (StVZO), Criminal Code (§ 315b/c StGB), and FGSV guidelines.
* **Accident Definition:** Any event resulting in death, injury, or property damage due to road traffic on public roads.
* **Key Metrics:**
  * **Accident Density ($UD$):** Accidents per kilometer per year ($U / (km \cdot a)$).
  * **Accident Rate ($UR$):** Accidents relative to vehicle mileage ($U / (10^6 \text{ veh} \cdot km)$).
  * **Accident Cost Density ($UKD$) & Rate ($UKR$):** Economic loss mapped to section length or vehicle mileage.
* **Trends:** Road transport accounts for the vast majority of transport-related casualties. While traffic volume has steadily increased over recent decades, fatalities have dropped significantly due to multi-layered safety measures.


## 2. Accident Causes and Influencing Factors
* **Human Errors:** Responsible for ~92% of personal injury accidents. Primary causes include inappropriate speed, failure to yield right-of-way, insufficient gap distance, DUI, and distraction. Speeding is prevalent among young drivers, while right-of-way errors peak in older demographics ($\ge65$).
* **Infrastructure & Environment:** Rural roads (*außerorts*) account for the highest proportion of fatalities (~58%), whereas urban areas (*innerorts*) see the highest rate of light injuries. Rear-end collisions at the end of traffic jams represent a major fatality hazard, especially involving heavy goods vehicles (HGVs). Weather (rain, fog) plays a minor role compared to dry conditions.
* **Vulnerable Road Users (VRUs):** Special considerations apply to pedestrians, cyclists, Pedelec users (who exhibit higher crash severity and control loss rates due to elevated speeds), and E-scooter riders.

## 3. Measures to Increase Traffic Safety

### Accident Analysis & Methodologies
* **Traffic Conflict Technique (*Verkehrskonflikttechnik*):** Standardized observation of non-crash near-miss interactions to analyze underlying risk patterns.
* **Data Collection:** Standardized police reporting (*Verkehrsunfallanzeige* - VUA) tracking accident types, categories, and environmental conditions.
* **Geospatial & Network Analysis:** Digital pin maps (*Unfall-Steckkarten*), collision diagrams (*Unfall-Diagramme*), and Network Safety Analysis (*ESN / SIPO*). The Safety Potential ($SIPO$) measures the difference between actual accident cost rates ($UKR$) and baseline rates ($gUKR$) achievable through guidelines-compliant infrastructure.

### Policy, Legislation & Education
* **Education & Enforcement:** School traffic programs, probation driver licensing (e.g., *Begleitetes Fahren ab 17*), stricter sanctions, and targeted public media campaigns.
* **Legislative Milestones:** Urban 50 km/h speed limits, rural 100 km/h limits, seatbelt mandates, strict blood alcohol concentration limits (0.5‰), and mandatory safety systems.
* **Vision Zero & Strategy:** Policy framework aiming to eliminate traffic fatalities by building human-tolerant infrastructure, enforcing vehicle safety, and encouraging user responsibility.

### Infrastructure Design & Traffic Control
* **Safety Audits (*RSAS*):** Formal multi-phase independent auditing across planning, design, execution, and post-opening phases.
* **Accident Commissions (*Unfallkommission*):** Interdisciplinary local groups (authorities, road operators, police) analyzing high-accident spots to implement structural fixes.
* **Design Principles:** Complete modal separation on high-capacity routes versus soft separation/shared spaces in residential zones.
* **Traffic Control Systems (VBA):** Dynamic speed adjustments, congestion warnings, ramp metering, and optimized signal timing to smooth traffic flow and reduce speed variances.

### Vehicle Technology & Connectivity
* **Active & Passive Systems:** ABS, ESP, lane-keeping assistance, crumple zones, airbags, and Euro NCAP crash-test rating standards.
* **V2X Communication & eCall:** Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) safety applications (e.g., red-light violation warnings, hazard messaging) paired with automated emergency calls (eCall).
