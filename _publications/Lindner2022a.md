summary: >
  Bicycle-to-automated-vehicle (AV) interaction is underrepresented in traffic research compared to pedestrian-AV studies. The authors built a coupled driving simulator linking an AV simulator with a real-bicycle simulator, using a web application for mobile human-machine interfaces letting the AV passenger set priority rules at conflict points, tested in a proof-of-concept study with 16 simulation runs. Overall impressions were rated positively: 3.20 for bicyclists, 3.75 for AV passengers, on a 5-point scale.
