summary: >
  This study examines how road infrastructure and human-machine interface (HMI) type affect cyclists' decisions to overtake or stop behind an automated shuttle bus. Fifty participants rode a CAVE-based bicycle simulator through scenarios varying bus stop and bicycle infrastructure and HMI design, with behavior analyzed via logistic regression. A Stop HMI significantly increased stopping (odds ratio 2.035, p=0.007), and bike lanes reduced stopping odds, though cyclists overtook in 64% of cases.
