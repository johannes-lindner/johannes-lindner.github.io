summary: >
  Electric cargo bikes could replace traditional urban delivery methods, but infrastructure designed for conventional bicycles limits adoption. Researchers surveyed 37 short-term test riders and tracked 12 long-term riders using four cargo bike models over two to three months, combining questionnaires with objective position data. Nearly 85% of short-term participants had never ridden a cargo bike, yet 70% were willing to use one, and riders wanted wider bike lanes, lowered curbs.
