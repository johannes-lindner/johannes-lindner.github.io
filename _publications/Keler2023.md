summary: >
  Urban digital twins need to convert road network data between incompatible geodata formats to support traffic simulation. The Digital Twin Munich project develops conversion techniques that transform the city's lane model into a routable network for the SUMO traffic simulator and link it to a Unity-based 3D virtual reality environment, tested with physical vulnerable-road-user simulators. Testing revealed lane-shape conversion artifacts and unrealistic simulated movement patterns, prompting converter adjustments.
