summary: >
  This study addresses how test environment choice affects observed driver behavior toward cyclists at right-turn intersections. Gaze behavior and subjective realism were compared between a test track study and simulator studies using a head-mounted-display simulator and an LED-wall simulator, replicating the same maneuver. Shoulder glances occurred more often on the test track, mirror glances more often in simulators, and simulators were rated more realistic than the test track.
