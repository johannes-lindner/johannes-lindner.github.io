summary: >
  Fully automated public transportation requires replacing tasks currently performed by human bus drivers, but how these translate into technical requirements is unclear. The authors analyze legal frameworks in Europe and North America and interview bus drivers to identify tasks such as passenger safety monitoring, assistance, and communication. They derive functional requirements and propose a Public Transport Infrastructure Control System (PT-ICS) integrating them into automated vehicle and infrastructure architecture.
