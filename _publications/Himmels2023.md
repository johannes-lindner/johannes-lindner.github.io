summary: >
  Prior research on head-mounted displays (HMDs) in driving simulation gives mixed evidence on simulator sickness, presence, and perception versus screens. A within-subject study with 31 participants compared a Varjo VR-3 HMD to an LED wall simulator using Bayesian hypothesis testing across scenarios measuring distance/speed perception, sickness, and presence. The HMD performed equivalently to the LED wall on sickness, presence, and active distance perception, with only anecdotal evidence of worse braking.
