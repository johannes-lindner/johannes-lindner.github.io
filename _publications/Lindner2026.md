summary: >
  This paper addresses how human bus driver tasks can be translated into functional requirements for automated, legally compliant public transit systems. The authors analyzed transport legislation in Germany, France, the USA, and Canada and conducted expert interviews with bus drivers in Munich. They identify six driver task categories and derive functional requirements structured within a Sense-Plan-Act model and Service-Oriented Architecture, proposed as a Public Transport Control System.
