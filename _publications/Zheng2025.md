summary: >
  Predicting cyclists' maneuvers before an intersection matters for automated vehicle safety, but prior methods needed external sensors or predefined gesture rules. The authors used video from 31 participants in a virtual reality (VR) bicycle simulator to train a two-stage bidirectional long short-term memory (B-LSTM) model that classifies gestures before predicting left turn, right turn, or crossing maneuvers. It outperformed a rule-based baseline, and implicit gestures with weightings improved accuracy most.
