summary: >
  Lane-free traffic (LFT), where connected and automated vehicles maneuver without lane markings to increase road capacity, has mainly been studied through two-dimensional simulations, leaving human behavior and perceived safety unexamined. The paper proposes a conceptual framework for human-in-the-loop LFT studies using a dynamic driving simulator, specifying required hardware and software components, and introduces a scenario matrix covering freeway, urban, and rural environments to guide future driving-simulator testing of LFT strategies.
