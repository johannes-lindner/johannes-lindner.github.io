summary: >
  Human-machine interface research for automated vehicles (AVs) has focused mainly on car-pedestrian interaction, leaving AV-bicyclist communication largely unaddressed. The authors built a mobile application letting an AV, its passenger, and a bicyclist negotiate right-of-way at intersections, tested in 16 runs on coupled driving simulators. Decisions took a median of 3.99 seconds, rising to 9.25 seconds with added traffic-impact information, and perceived safety at conflict points increased significantly with app use.
