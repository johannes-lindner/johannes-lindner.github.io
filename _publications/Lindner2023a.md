summary: >
  Existing wheelchair simulators for studying driving behavior and road-user interaction typically use a static, fixed-base design. The authors built a real-motion virtual reality wheelchair simulator with a wireless HTC VIVE headset, VR trackers on the wheelchair's wheels, and a Unity 3D environment, letting users move freely while only visual stimuli are replaced or augmented. Tracking wheelchair and headset separately is novel, though tracking space is limited to about 10x10 m.
