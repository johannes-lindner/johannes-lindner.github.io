summary: >
  This study addresses the unclear validity of bicycle simulators by testing whether active steering (force feedback) improves correspondence to real cycling behavior. A CAVE (Cave Automatic Virtual Environment) bicycle simulator with a fitness bike was used in a within-subjects study of 21 participants performing lane-change maneuvers with and without active steering. Active steering significantly reduced steering angle oscillation, especially at lower speeds, while trajectory oscillation showed no significant difference between conditions.
