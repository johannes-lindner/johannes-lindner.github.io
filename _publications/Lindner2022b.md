summary: >
  Cargo bicycle riders are often omitted from driving simulators used in transportation planning, despite cargo bikes differing from conventional bicycles in weight, size, and load. The paper presents a virtual reality electric cargo bicycle simulator using a sensor-equipped trike, VR headset, and Unity 3D, tested with 18 subjects. Results showed riding without electric assistance was easier, and motion sickness occurred frequently among untrained VR users.
