This week, our colleagues Johannes Lindner, Isabella Waldorf, and Santiago Álvarez-Ossorio Martínez traveled to Vienna to present their research at the 17th International Conference on Travel Behavior Research (IATBR International Conference on Travel Behavior). Their presentations focused on the needs of transit users with visual impairment and autism spectrum disorder, the effects of low-fare public transport fares in Germany on marginalized individuals, and the mode choice behavior in a Tradeable Mobility Credit Scheme, respectively.
 
The conference was a fantastic opportunity to meet with over 300 researchers from all around the world and discuss the latest developments in the field. Besides, despite the 🔥 temperatures, it also allowed the participants to enjoy some of the best spots in the Austrian capital (thanks to BOKU University and Yusak Susilo's team for the fantastic organization). 

Looking forward to the next IATBR 2027!
