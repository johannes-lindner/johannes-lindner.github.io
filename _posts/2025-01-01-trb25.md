TRBAM 2025 has concluded, and we are already looking forward to the next one!

This year, our team proudly represented Europe and Technische Universität München at the world’s largest transport event, with 27 accepted papers, 9 lectern presentations, 18 posters, and 20 chair members attending.

We had a great time connecting with people, engaging in research discussions, and showcasing our work. All in all, we consider this year’s Transportation Research Board Annual Meeting Hashtag#TRBAM a success, and we would like to thank everyone who contributed in it!
