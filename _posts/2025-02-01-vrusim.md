Recently, Tian Zheng and Johannes Lindner took part in the 𝟰𝘁𝗵 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻-𝗖𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗩𝗥𝗨 𝗦𝗶𝗺𝘂𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝘀𝗵𝗼𝗽 in Linköping, Sweden. It was a great chance to connect with researchers from across Europe and dive into the latest in simulating vulnerable road users (VRUs) like pedestrians and cyclists.
 
As simulation technology becomes more advanced, it plays an increasingly important role in creating safer, more inclusive mobility systems. The workshop—hosted by Swedish National Road and Transport Research Institute (VTI) and Deutsches Zentrum für Luft-und Raumfahrt e.V.—showcased numerous impressive simulation setups and demos, including VTI’s bicycle simulator, XR/VR pedestrian environments, and the SIM III full-scale driving simulator.

We would like to thank the organizers and everyone from Linköpings universitet, Lund University, Kungliga Tekniska högskolan, and the other institutions for the engaging discussions and insights. We’re looking forward to continuing the conversation and driving human-centered mobility forward!
