A recent study by the 𝗙𝗲𝗱𝗲𝗿𝗮𝗹 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗢𝗳𝗳𝗶𝗰𝗲 (https://lnkd.in/d-tzgJyb) highlights a worrying trend: in 2024, the number of e-scooter accidents involving personal injury rose by 26.7% compared to the previous year.

Our research team is deeply engaged in studying the challenges of micromobility and traffic safety. Last week, our colleagues Johannes Lindner, Ana-Maria Oraviţan, and our student Maximilian Kolb presented our latest research tool, the E-Scooter Simulator, in interviews with BR - Bayerischer Rundfunk. They also discussed the latest accident statistics in short news segments for the BR24 program.
