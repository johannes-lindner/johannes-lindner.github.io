Congratulations to our Master's thesis student Abhay Joshi for receiving an award from the German Association of Engineers for his Thesis work!

His thesis focused on developing a control system for fully automated public transport, leveraging cooperative perception to enhance traffic safety, efficiency, and passenger comfort. Supervised by Mario Ilic and Johannes Lindner, this work was part of the OBACHT research project. 

We look forward to continuing collaboration on this interesting topic!
