We had the pleasure of hosting the combined 𝟱𝘁𝗵 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻-𝗖𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗩𝗥𝗨 𝗦𝗶𝗺𝘂𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝘀𝗵𝗼𝗽 and the 𝟭𝘀𝘁 𝗖𝘆𝗰𝗹𝗶𝘀𝘁 𝗕𝗲𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗶𝗼𝗿 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝘀𝗵𝗼𝗽! 

Hosted at the Vorhoelzer Forum at the Technische Universität München, this combined event was organized by our team, led by Johannes Lindner, in close collaboration with our co-organizing partners, Martin Fischer (Deutsches Zentrum für Luft-und Raumfahrt e.V.) and Heather Kaths (Bergische Universität Wuppertal).

The event was specifically designed with an informal setting, and it was incredible to see the detailed discussions, networking, and collaborative spirit that emerged!

We also want to extend our gratitude to the AgiMo – TRR 408 ( Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) - German Research Foundation, DFG-531327426) for supporting these workshops.

Looking forward to next year's event!
