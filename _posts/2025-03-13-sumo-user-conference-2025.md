From 12 May to 14, together with my colleagues Mario Ilic, Evald Nexhipi, and Mathias Pechinger, I attended the **Sumo User Conference 2025** in Berlin. While Evald, Mario, and Mathias presented three posters on SumoWare, a tool that bridges SUMO and Autoware for realistic autonomous vehicle evaluation, FTO-Sim, our open-source framework for floating traffic observation, and our latest Sumonity updates for 2025, including improvements in pedestrian modeling and streamlined deployment. I gave a presentation on modeling passenger boarding times using Sumonity's submicroscopic pedestrian simulation. Overall, it was a great event to connect with the community and share ideas!
